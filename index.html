Frequently Asked Questions & Visiting Guide

How many National Trust properties are in this directory?

This interactive explorer indexes all 678 National Trust visitor properties, estates, historic houses, gardens, castles, and open coastlines across England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, powered by official National Trust GIS data.

How do I find National Trust places near me?

Tap Change next to Origin in the top bar to choose Use My Current GPS Location or enter your UK town or postcode. The app recalculates road driving distances and travel times, sorting places from nearest to furthest.

How are driving times and distances calculated?

Driving distances account for typical UK road network routing factors (1.25x straight-line distance factor with tiered motorway and rural speed modelling). Every card also includes a 1-click turn-by-turn navigation button to Google Maps.

What are typical National Trust opening times?

Gardens and parklands typically open from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, historic houses from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and countryside or coastal areas are accessible dawn-to-dusk. Tap Details on any place card to check live opening schedules.
